# leave_policy_id and gender_restriction do not exist — so LeaveService works

# This is most of tests/Fixtures/model-columns.txt as well: seven of the
# models listed there are the second model on a table already listed here.
# Resolving the duplicate resolves the phantom columns with it.
#
# Which model survives is a decision per table, not a rename. A ratchet: the
# list may shrink and may not grow, and the test fails if an entry here has
# been resolved and not removed.
